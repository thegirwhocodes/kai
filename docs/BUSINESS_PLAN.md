# Kai Focus Business Plan

## One-line thesis

Kai Focus is an adaptive AI focus coach: a beautiful Pomodoro-style workspace that plans the user's next focus block around calendar pressure, inbox signals, energy patterns, Spotify music, and long-term productivity trends.

## Positioning

Flocus sells calm focus aesthetics and lightweight stats. Sunsama, Akiflow, Reclaim, and Motion sell planning and calendar control. Brain.fm sells functional focus audio. Kai Focus should sit between those worlds:

- Calm visual focus room.
- Adaptive session timing instead of fixed 25/5.
- Voice-first personal operations layer.
- Calendar, Gmail, tasks, and music as planning signals.
- Trends that learn when the user actually locks in.

Public mark: **Kai Focus**. Keep "Kai" as the assistant's familiar name, but use the fuller mark for SEO, domain, trademark clearance, and category clarity.

## Product pillars

1. **Focus Room**
   - Large clock/timer, visual backgrounds, task list, start/break controls.
   - Free tier should make the product feel immediately useful.

2. **Adaptive Timing**
   - Session length adjusts from recent focus ratings, streak, calendar space, and break history.
   - Do not market 25/5 as scientifically optimal.

3. **Life-Aware Planning**
   - Google Calendar forward/backward search.
   - Gmail search and draft editing.
   - Priorities and tasks weighted by Sabi/Education for Equality, school, family, deadlines, and admin.

4. **Kai Trends**
   - Paid flagship.
   - Energy map, best focus windows, calendar fragmentation, music fit, interruption rate, session completion, break balance, and weekly reports.

5. **Kai Lock-In Radio**
   - Spotify control first.
   - Later: licensed/generative instrumental focus streams.
   - Music should be measured against focus outcomes, not treated as decorative background.

## Pricing

Recommended launch model:

- **Free**: timer, basic tasks, manual focus ratings, basic daily stats, limited backgrounds, text/voice control.
- **Kai Plus**: 30-day trial, then `$9/mo` or `$72/yr`.
- **Founding annual**: `$49-$59/yr` for early users.
- **Founding beta**: 90 days free only for testers/interviewees who give feedback.

Why not 90 days for everyone:

- Trends need enough data to prove value, so no-free is too harsh.
- A 7-day trial is too short for behavior analytics.
- 30 days is the best public default because it lets users build a real week-over-week pattern without delaying monetization for a full quarter.

## What to add

- Real onboarding: connect calendar, connect Spotify, set priorities, add first task, choose work mode.
- Public landing page and SEO pages.
- Pricing page.
- Privacy Policy and Terms.
- Google OAuth verification readiness.
- In-app Trends dashboard.
- Weekly email/report export.
- Test/demo mode with fake calendar and inbox data.
- Feedback widget.
- Analytics events for activation and retention.

## What to remove or avoid

- A settings wall copied from Flocus.
- Too many theme-only paid features.
- Generic quotes as a main feature.
- Unsupported "energy dip" claims.
- Bare "Kai" as the public business mark.
- AI music claims without licensing/commercial rights.
- Asking random testers to connect Gmail before privacy, OAuth review, and demo data are ready.

## Launch requirements

1. Buy a category-clear domain, preferably `kaifocus.com` if registrar checkout confirms availability.
2. Add custom domain in Vercel.
3. Create support email and business inbox.
4. Publish Privacy Policy, Terms, OAuth data-use disclosure, and support/contact pages.
5. Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
6. Create Product Hunt, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, and student productivity launch material.
7. Recruit 30-50 testers before charging broadly.
8. Form LLC and get EIN before meaningful paid launch.
9. Run trademark clearance before filing.
