# Ads and Testing Research

## Testing strategy

Start with free/low-cost testers before paid ads. Kai needs retention proof more than traffic.

Best tester sources:

- Wesleyan/student networks.
- Indian student communities on Reddit, Discord, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and college entrepreneurship groups.
- Productivity/ADHD/study communities.
- Indie Hackers, Product Hunt Ship, BetaList, and founder communities.
- TikTok/Instagram short demos asking for 25 beta testers.

Can we get users from India to test for free?

Yes, but make the ask respectful and specific: "free Plus for 90 days in exchange for two feedback forms and one optional 20-minute interview." India has a huge English-speaking student/tech audience, but do not frame it as cheap labor. Give real value, protect privacy, and use demo mode before asking for Gmail/Calendar access.

Recommended beta cohort:

- 10 US college students.
- 10 Indian students preparing for exams/projects.
- 10 founders/builders.
- 10 ADHD/productivity users.
- 5 remote workers with heavy calendars.

What to test:

- Activation: can they start a focus block within 60 seconds?
- Planner value: do they accept Kai's suggested next block?
- Trust: do they understand why Kai chose the block?
- Music: can they successfully ask for a Spotify playlist?
- Trends: do they return to check patterns after 3 days?
- Retention: D1, D3, D7.
- Willingness to pay: after seeing Trends, would they pay `$9/mo`, `$6/mo`, or annual only?

## Free testing setup

- Ship demo mode with sample calendar/email.
- Add a feedback form after every third completed block.
- Add a "send feedback" button in the app.
- Offer 90-day Founding Plus for testers.
- Recruit through a simple landing page: "Become a Kai Focus founding tester."

## Paid ads strategy

Do not start with large ad spend. Use ads to test messages, not scale.

### Search ads

Google Search is useful for high-intent phrases:

- "pomodoro timer"
- "focus timer"
- "ai productivity coach"
- "study timer"
- "calendar planner app"
- "focus music app"
- "adhd focus timer"

Run exact/phrase match first. Send traffic to a dedicated landing page, not directly to `/app`.

Test copy:

- "AI Pomodoro coach that plans your next block."
- "A focus timer that learns your best hours."
- "Calendar-aware focus sessions with Spotify."

### TikTok/Reels/Shorts

Best for showing the product:

- "I asked my focus app what to do next."
- "My calendar was chaos, so Kai picked the only block that fit."
- "Hey Kai, play Christian lofi and start 25 minutes."

### Reddit

Use carefully. Communities hate drive-by ads. Better:

- Ask for beta testers.
- Share build-in-public posts.
- Post in allowed self-promotion threads.
- Target paid ads only after organic response is positive.

### Influencers

Micro-creators are probably better than broad ads:

- StudyTok.
- Med/law students.
- ADHD productivity creators.
- Christian study/lofi creators.
- Founder/productivity YouTubers.

## Budget

First month:

- `$0-$150`: organic beta recruitment.
- `$300-$500`: small creative tests on TikTok/Instagram.
- `$300-$500`: Google Search keyword tests.
- `$100-$300`: one or two micro-creator tests.

Kill any channel that cannot produce activated users, not just clicks.
